canonical shared skills under `.github/skills/`.

discovery adapters. Do not edit them directly; update the canonical `.github`
asset and run:

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File ./sync_ai_assets.ps1
```

are not production-ready, and no JFrog, SonarQube, or private enterprise
registry assumptions apply.
